# Third-party assets, trademark, and distribution rights

SEAF Companion is **unofficial** and currently wraps the website [seaf.se](https://seaf.se/).

The source tree currently contains or refers to:
- A SEAF-themed launcher icon, supplied for this personal project. **Public redistribution and trademark permissions have not been verified.**
- Embedded SEAF game images/content served by the third-party website. Those assets are **not licensed by virtue of their visibility in WebView**.
- A custom mission notification audio file labelled as a Helldivers 2 clip, supplied for personal experimentation. **Rights to redistribute it publicly are not established.** Replace/remove it or obtain appropriate permission before publishing binaries or exposing the repository.
- Android and Gradle tooling, subject to their respective licenses.

**Do not publish to Google Play or make third-party binary assets public until rights have been documented.** A generic non-infringing notification sound and independently created icon are safer distribution defaults.

Before releasing, document: original authors, the rights holder, license/permission, attribution requirements and any use limitations for each asset.
