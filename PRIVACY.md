# SEAF Companion Privacy Notice (draft)

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This notice describes SEAF Companion's **Android wrapper code**. It does not cover the independent website at https://seaf.se/ or services integrated by that website.

## Data handled by the Android app
- **Website data:** The built-in Android WebView loads seaf.se and its linked services. Those services may process sign-in details, identifiers, cookies, gameplay state, and network requests under their own policies.
- **Local preferences:** The app stores manually scheduled reminder deadlines and display settings locally on the device.
- **Notifications:** Android receives reminders, using the content generated by the app; notification availability and sound depend on device settings.
- **Permissions:** INTERNET to load the game, POST_NOTIFICATIONS to alert users on supported Android versions, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to restore pending reminders after restart.

The current Android project does not include an independent advertising or analytics SDK or a separate account database. That statement does not describe what the third-party website collects.

## Data sharing and retention
The Android wrapper does not intentionally transmit local reminder settings to a developer-controlled server. Website content and authentication requests are handled by the relevant websites. Clear SEAF Companion's app data or uninstall the application to remove its locally stored preferences and WebView data. Clearing app data may sign you out.

## Contact and rights
**Before public release:** Add a verified publisher contact email and a public, accessible support website. Complete an end-to-end privacy/data-flow audit and align this notice with Google Play Data Safety disclosures and the actual third-party site behavior.

This is a **draft**, not a finalized compliant privacy policy. Google Play requires a publicly accessible web-hosted privacy policy linked in both the store listing and app.
